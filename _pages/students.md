Our group brings together students and researchers interested in semiconductor devices, process technology, integration, and design–technology co-optimization. We value curiosity, rigorous thinking, collaboration, and a willingness to take ownership of difficult problems.

## Join the group

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students and postdoctoral researchers who are excited by real challenges in semiconductor technology. Research topics may include advanced transistor architectures, CFET and vertical devices, Flip FET and 3D integration, DTCO, and manufacturing-aware technology development.

**What we look for**
- Curiosity about how semiconductor devices and integrated circuits work.
- A strong willingness to learn, reason carefully, and solve open-ended problems.
- Persistence when experiments, simulations, or first ideas do not work.
- Clear communication and a collaborative approach to research.

You do not need to know everything before joining us. A strong foundation and a desire to learn are an excellent starting point.

**How to inquire:** Email **hengwu at pku.edu.cn** with your CV, academic background, research interests, and a short introduction explaining why you would like to join the group. Please include the position or degree program you are interested in and your expected availability.
