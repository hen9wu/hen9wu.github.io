# Collaborate with the DTCO Device Lab

We welcome research conversations with universities, research institutes, semiconductor manufacturers, equipment and materials companies, and EDA or design teams. We are particularly interested in collaborations that connect promising device concepts with integration, design, and manufacturing realities.

## Areas of shared interest

- **Advanced transistor architectures:** CFET, vertical FETs, and emerging device structures.
- **3D and dual-sided integration:** Flip FET, Flip 3D, backside interconnects, and related integration challenges.
- **Design–technology co-optimization:** Device and process trade-offs, layout, parasitics, standard-cell design, and circuit-level evaluation.
- **Semiconductor manufacturing:** Process integration, manufacturability, and technology evaluation.

## Ways to work together

Depending on the research question, collaboration may involve joint research proposals, technical exchanges, co-authored publications, student research, or focused studies of a specific device or integration challenge. The appropriate scope, deliverables, data access, intellectual-property arrangements, and publication plans can be discussed at the outset.

## Start a conversation

To help us understand the opportunity, please email **hengwu at pku.edu.cn** and include:

1. A brief description of the technical challenge or research idea.
2. The potential contribution or expertise of your team.
3. The type of collaboration you have in mind.
4. Any relevant public papers, technical materials, or project context.

We value technically grounded discussions and collaborations built around clear questions, rigorous evidence, and outcomes that advance semiconductor technology.
